Shipping Delay Policy
If delivery is late, apologize to the customer and tell them the support team will check the delivery status.

Damaged Item Policy
If an item or package is damaged, apologize and ask the customer to share a photo so replacement can be reviewed.

Refund Policy
If the customer asks for a refund, acknowledge the request and tell them refund eligibility will be reviewed.

Return Policy
Items can be returned within 30 days if unused and in original condition.

Escalation Rule
If the customer mentions legal, safety, injury, fraud, or repeated unresolved issue, escalate to the support team.
